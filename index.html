Support

Help and contact for the Mac apps listed below.

Dictavoice

Dictation for macOS. Hold a hotkey, speak, and the text lands on your clipboard. Speech recognition runs on your Mac by default.

CleanBarQuick

Hides the menu bar icons you choose, with a single click. No special permissions needed.

Getting help

Questions, bug reports and feature requests are welcome. Reach the developer via github.com/prutser0.

Common questions

Dictavoice transcribes nothing. Open Settings from the menu bar icon and check that a speech model is installed under “Speech Model”. Without one, on-device transcription has nothing to run.

The app has no window. Both apps live in the menu bar and have no Dock icon. Look for their icon at the top right of the screen.

Why is the orange microphone dot always on? Dictavoice watches your microphone level so the menu bar icon can react when you speak. It reads loudness only — nothing is recorded or sent. Quit Dictavoice and the dot goes away.

CleanBarQuick will not hide an icon. Hold ⌘ and drag the icons you want hidden to the left of the chevron. The clock and Control Center are pinned by macOS and cannot be hidden.
