Privacy Policy

Applies to Dictavoice and CleanBarQuick for macOS.

Last updated: 23 August 2026

The short version

Neither app collects, stores, or transmits personal information to the developer. There are no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no user accounts.

Dictavoice — audio

By default, Dictavoice transcribes speech entirely on your device using a local speech recognition model. Audio never leaves your Mac.

If you enable the OpenAI backend in Settings, the audio you record is sent to OpenAI for the sole purpose of transcription, and the resulting text is returned to your Mac. This happens only while that setting is enabled and only with an API key you provide yourself. Your use of that service is governed by OpenAI’s privacy policy.

Dictavoice does not retain recordings. Audio is held in memory during a recording and discarded afterwards, unless you explicitly use “Save Last Recording” to write a file to a location you choose.

Dictavoice — API keys

If you supply an OpenAI API key, it is stored in the macOS Keychain on your device and is transmitted only to OpenAI.

Dictavoice — microphone and system audio

Dictavoice requests microphone access to record speech, and audio capture access for the optional system audio transcription feature.

The menu bar icon reacts when Dictavoice can hear you. To do that, the app measures the input level of your microphone continuously while it is running — which is why macOS shows the orange microphone indicator even when you are not dictating. Only loudness is read. Nothing is recorded, kept, or sent anywhere during this monitoring.

Audio is captured and transcribed only while you hold a shortcut yourself. System audio is captured only while you hold the system audio shortcut.

CleanBarQuick

CleanBarQuick shows and hides menu bar icons. It requires no special permissions, has no network access, and stores nothing beyond a single local preference recording whether icons are currently hidden.

Children

Neither app is directed at children and neither collects personal information from anyone.
